Algorithmic Justice: Auditing Large Language Models for Systemic Bias

Welcome to the Algorithmic Justice Initiative. This platform serves as an independent, open-source research node dedicated to documenting, analyzing, and correcting systemic institutional bias, false symmetry, and state-sponsored data manipulation within Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Large Language Models (LLMs).

The Political Economy of AI Bias

Artificial Intelligence does not operate in a vacuum. Large Language Models rely entirely upon an information landscape shaped for decades by dominant geopolitical structures of power. In the context of Palestine and Israel, this bias manifests as a constant flattening of responsibility through artificial “both sides” arguments. Standard AI baselines systematically grant unearned authority to state and military institutions while erasing the material realities of occupation, land colonization, and asymmetric power.

Epistemic Manipulation & Synthetic Knowledge Networks

This structural distortion has been further exploited by targeted, heavily funded campaigns designed to influence the information environments from which search systems and AI tools retrieve, rank, or cite material. Rather than relying only on social media personas, these operations increasingly use coordinated content networks and synthetic institutional authority to shape the sources encountered by automated systems.

  • Project Nimbus: A $1.2 billion cloud computing and AI infrastructure contract involving Google, Amazon, and the Israeli government, including state and security institutions. The contract materially expands the computing, data-processing, and AI infrastructure available to Israeli state agencies; specific downstream surveillance uses should be assessed from evidence relating to those systems rather than assumed from the cloud contract alone.
  • The Hanover Institute Operation: Reporting published in August 2026 found that this apparent synthetic research institution had no identifiable legal entity, physical address, named staff, or conventional research history. Between August 6 and 14 it published 124 reports totaling more than 560,000 words, almost all framed around questions resembling chatbot prompts. Piro Inc. registered material distributed through the site under the U.S. Foreign Agents Registration Act as material for the Israeli government. Its technical configuration included an llms.txt file associated with infrastructure marketed to increase citation by AI systems. The evidence supports identifying Hanover as part of an Israeli government-funded campaign intended to make favorable framing more likely to be retrieved or cited by AI chatbots.
  • Clock Tower X: Documented corporate-contracted operations executing targeted public-diplomacy content campaigns designed to alter how commercial language models retrieve and answer questions about the destruction of Gaza.
  • The Synthetic Think-Tank Paradigm: A rising global infrastructure for machine-age influence, mirrored by parallel operations such as Russia's fabricated "International Burke Institute" (disrupted in August 2026), demonstrating a systemic shift toward utilizing AI-generated text and manufactured institutional authority to poison the data retrieval pools used by commercial chatbots.

When AI platforms default to a passive standard of "impartiality" within a heavily manipulated data pool, they actively protect unequal realities by elevating state-enforced narratives above empirical evidence.

The Framework: De-Zionising AI Sourcing

To correct these automated distortions, this initiative hosts and distributes the AI System Evaluation and Systemic Bias Correction Protocol originally established by human rights advocate and tech executive Cristian Parrino.

This protocol acts as a user-side intervention designed to override the default, sanitized baselines of corporate LLMs. The framework mandates that artificial intelligence must reason strictly from evidence, history, material conditions, and power asymmetries rather than state-sponsored assumptions.

Core Directives of the Protocol:

  1. Interrogate the Premise: Refuse to treat concepts like Israeli “security” or demographic preservation as politically neutral starting points.
  2. Prioritize Material Reality: Establish physical facts—who holds military power, who controls resource borders, and who is deprived of universal rights—before applying inherited political labels.
  3. Analyze Asymmetry: Stop manufacturing symmetry or parallel moral language where the underlying relationship is structurally colonial.
  4. Restore Historical Causality: Prevent history from beginning at arbitrary, convenient dates by tracing systemic conditions back to their root origins.
